About Us
At Cryotherapy Rejuvenate, we are dedicated to redefining wellness through innovation, care, and expertise.
Nestled in Dickson City, PA,
our facility serves the Northeastern Pennsylvania (NEPA) region with advanced cryotherapy treatments tailored to enhance recovery, rejuvenation, and overall well-being.
Our Mission
Our mission is to
empower individuals of all ages
and fitness levels to achieve their personal health and wellness goals. Whether you’re a student-athlete, a busy professional, or a senior seeking improved mobility, we strive to offer a welcoming environment where cutting-edge technology meets personalized care. Every visit is a step towards helping you feel stronger, healthier, and revitalized.
Our Team of Experts
The owners of Cryo Therapy Rejuvenate are Mike Amory and Mark Heise.
Mike
has been in the medical and healthcare industry since 1987. He began his career with Johnson & Johnson as a sales representative/specialist in operating rooms in hospitals. Mike started his own medical equipment business in 2003 and treated many patients in NEPA.
He has witnessed and traveled with a University baseball and football team for the last 15 years and noticed the need for a better way for the athletes to restore and recover their muscles…
Mike also owns Cryotherapy Restore & Rejuvenate located in Dallas, Pa.
Mike Amory
Mark Heise is a dedicated owner and partner at Cryotherapy Rejuvenate, where he brings over 24 years of experience in customer service and logistics to the wellness industry. Known for his strong commitment to client care, Mark combines his operational expertise with a passion for helping others feel their best. His background in managing high-level customer interactions and complex logistics has shaped his approach to delivering smooth, supportive, and results-driven experiences for every client who walks through the door. Mark’s leadership continues to guide Cryotherapy Rejuvenate in providing innovative, restorative services that enhance overall well-being.
Mark Heise
Mike and Mark want to bring this high-tech whole-body cryotherapy to NEPA so athletes and everyone else who experiences pain and inflammation will get relief.
Our dedicated team combines safety, expertise, and a passion for wellness to ensure every session is tailored to your goals. From the moment you arrive, we’re here to answer your questions and guide you through each step of your wellness experience.
Advanced Technology
for Your Recovery
Cryotherapy Rejuvenate offers world-class innovation with the MECOTEC cryotherapy chamber and cryo air portable system for localized treatments.
The MECOTEC
Cryo System
Takes cryotherapy to a more targeted level with localized treatments. Notably, this system also includes a facemask option, ideal for skin rejuvenation. By enhancing skin tone, promoting collagen production, and reducing puffiness and wrinkles, the Iceberg Cryo facemask helps clients achieve a radiant, youthful glow. From specific injury recovery to revitalizing your skin, this flexible treatment system is here to meet your needs.
The MECOTEC
Cryotherapy Chamber
Provides fully immersive, whole-body cryotherapy. This MECOTEC system is trusted globally for its safety, efficiency, and ability to deliver consistent and comfortable results. Whether you’re seeking faster muscle recovery, pain relief, or a metabolism boost, this chamber is designed to cater to your unique wellness needs.

Cryotherapy is a cutting-edge treatment that uses extreme cold to support your body’s natural healing process.
